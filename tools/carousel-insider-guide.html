Slide 2 — Blausee

01
Bern Region
Blausee —
The Blue Lake
A crystal-clear alpine lake hidden in the Kander Valley. The water is so transparent you can see 15 meters to the bottom.
Insider tip: Visit in autumn when the forest turns golden and the crowds disappear.

Slide 3 — Bisse de Clavau

02
Valais
Bisse de Clavau —
Vineyard Trail
An ancient water channel trail through UNESCO-protected terraced vineyards of Sion. End the walk at a local cave for wine tasting.
Insider tip: Go at sunset. The light on the vineyards is unreal — and most caves stay open until 7pm.

Slide 4 — Seelisberg

03
Central Switzerland
Seelisberg —
Rütli Meadow
The birthplace of Switzerland, accessible only by boat or foot. A sacred meadow overlooking Lake Lucerne where the Swiss Confederation was born in 1291.
Insider tip: Take the boat from Brunnen. The approach by water is the way the original Swiss did it.

Slide 5 — Carouge

04
Geneva
Carouge —
Little Italy
A bohemian neighborhood that feels like Turin transplanted into Switzerland. Artisan workshops, the best aperitivo in the country — zero tour groups.
Insider tip: Wed & Sat mornings have an incredible market in Place du Marché. Arrive before 10am.

Slide 6 — Caumasee

05
Graubünden
Caumasee —
The Floating Lake
A turquoise lake near Flims fed entirely by underground springs, giving it an otherworldly color. Locals swim here in summer; in winter, it freezes into a natural ice rink.
Insider tip: Walk from Flims through the forest. The reveal when you first see the water is worth it.

Slide 7 — Corippo

06
Ticino
Corippo —
Smallest Village
Population: 12 people. A medieval stone village in the Verzasca Valley, now converted into a single scattered hotel across the old stone houses.
Insider tip: Combine with a swim in the emerald Verzasca River pools. The James Bond dam is 20 min away.

Slide 8 — Creux du Van

07
Vaud / Neuchâtel
Creux du Van —
Swiss Grand Canyon
A 160-meter vertical rock amphitheater in the Jura mountains. Wild ibex roam the cliff edges. A fraction of the people you'd find in the Alps.
Insider tip: Start from Noiraigue station. Pack a fondue kit and eat at the edge — the Swiss way.

Slide 9 — Hürlimann Spa

08
Zürich
Hürlimann Spa —
Rooftop Thermal Pool
A rooftop infinity thermal pool in a converted brewery, overlooking the Zürich skyline. 36°C water while the city lights up below.
Insider tip: Book Monday evening — least crowded. Pair with dinner at Clouds (same building, 35th floor).
