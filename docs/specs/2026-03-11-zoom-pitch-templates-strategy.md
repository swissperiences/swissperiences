# Zoom Intro Pitch Templates — Swissperiences

**Date**: 2026-03-11
**Context**: Zoom networking chats, English, mixed audience
**Moment**: Intro round (30-60 seconds)
**Objective**: Generate curiosity → people come talk to you after

---

## Structure (all templates)

| Part | Duration | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 5-10s | Capture attention (the line that makes heads turn) |
| **Bridge** | 10-15s | Minimal context (what you do, no over-explaining) |
| **Close** | 5-10s | Open loop (they WANT to ask more) |

### Rules

- Never mention prices
- Never say "luxury" — show it, don't say it
- Never sound like a sales pitch — sound like an interesting person sharing what they do
- "Swissperiences" appears once, naturally
- The close is never an explicit CTA — it's a line that triggers "tell me more"

---

## Template A — "The Anti-Tourism Hook"

**Best for**: Rooms with travelers, lifestyle people, anyone who's been to Switzerland and did the basic version.

**Hook:**
> "Most people visit Switzerland to check a box. I help people actually *feel* it."

**Bridge:**
> "I run a company called Swissperiences — we curate private stays and alpine experiences for a small group of members. No itineraries, no crowds. Just the real Switzerland most tourists never find."

**Close:**
> "If you ever think about spending time in Switzerland — not visiting, but actually spending time — I'm easy to find."

---

## Template B — "The Personal Story"

**Best for**: Rooms where people share personal stories, founder-friendly audiences, more intimate settings.

**Hook:**
> "I moved to Switzerland a few years ago looking for one thing — silence. I found it at 1,300 meters."

**Bridge:**
> "That experience changed me so much I built a company around it. Swissperiences — we host people in private alpine sanctuaries and curate the kind of Swiss moments you can't book on any platform."

**Close:**
> "It started as a personal obsession. Turns out a lot of people are looking for the same thing."

---

## Template C — "The Elegant One-Liner"

**Best for**: Large rooms, fast intro rounds, 20-30 seconds max. The pause after the one-liner does the heavy lifting.

**Hook:**
> "I sell silence in the Swiss Alps."
>
> *(pause — let it land)*

**Bridge:**
> "Swissperiences — we curate private mountain stays and experiences for a small membership. Think of it as the opposite of tourism."

**Close:**
> "Happy to chat with anyone curious about Switzerland beyond the postcards."

---

## Follow-up Lines (when someone asks "tell me more")

Use these as natural conversation continuations after the intro:

- "We keep it really small on purpose — right now it's a curated community of people who want depth, not distance."
- "The idea is simple: I found the most beautiful, quiet corners of Switzerland and I open them up to a handful of people each season."
- "We handle everything — the stay, the experiences, even cinematic documentation if you want it. You just show up and breathe."
- "The best way to see it is the site — swissperiences.ch. But honestly, just message me. That's how most people start."
