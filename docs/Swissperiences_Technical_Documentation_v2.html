1. Executive Summary

Swissperiences is a membership-based luxury travel platform for Switzerland operating a dual business model: curated alpine experiences for invite-only members, and short-term rental property management in Geneva. The platform is a fully custom Vite + React + TypeScript SPA (100% custom code) backed by Supabase PostgreSQL, Vercel serverless functions, Stripe payments, and Resend transactional emails.

Live at: swissperiences.ch — Domain registered through 2029 (Infomaniak)

2. Dual Business Model

2.1 Experience Curation (Primary)

Invite-only luxury travel platform offering curated alpine experiences across Switzerland. Members apply for access, receive approval, and book packages, standalone experiences, and sanctuary stays through a private dashboard. Revenue streams: package bookings, standalone experiences, retreat deposits, guest upsells (Airbnb), GetYourGuide affiliate commissions.

2.2 Property Management (New in v2.0)

Short-term rental management targeting Geneva properties. Commission-based model at 15–25% of rental revenue, operating within Switzerland's 90-night/year regulatory limit.

8. Authentication Flow

8.1 Google OAuth (Primary)

User clicks "Login with Google" → Supabase redirects to Google consent screen → Google redirects to /auth/callback → onAuthStateChange listener fires → Calls get_or_create_member() RPC → Routes by membership status:

StatusRedirect
active/members
pending/pending-approval
no_application/request-access

8.2 Email/Password (Secondary)

User enters email + password on expandable form at /login → signInWithPassword() to Supabase Auth → Same get_or_create_member() RPC routing.

8.3 AuthGuard Component

Wraps all protected routes. Checks: (1) auth.getUser() — is user authenticated? (2) get_or_create_member() RPC — membership status? (3) is_admin() RPC — if requireAdmin flag, checks admin_emails table. Admin access is email-based whitelist.

10. API Routes (Vercel Serverless)

10.1 POST /api/create-checkout-session

Creates Stripe Checkout Session for retreat deposits (CHF 500 / auto-detects GBP). Inputs: email (required), tier, intent, application_id, marketing_opt_in. Looks up waitlist entry, creates session, syncs to Resend audience.

10.2 POST /api/webhooks/stripe

Handles checkout.session.completed event. Verifies webhook signature, updates waitlist payment_status to "paid", sends confirmation email.

10.3 POST /api/send-inquiry-email

Corporate team retreat inquiry handler. Rate limited: 3 req / 10 min. Saves to corporate_inquiries, sends user confirmation + admin alert, syncs to Resend audience.

10.4 POST /api/send-guest-inquiry

Airbnb guest upsell from /enhance. Validates selections server-side (never trusts client totals). Server-side price catalog:

15. Legal Status — Sàrl Incorporation

INCORPORATION IN PROGRESS

Swissperiences Sàrl is currently being incorporated in Geneva through Startups.ch (Order #1484402). Legal advisor: Samy Benzaoui, Titulaire du Brevet d'avocat, Directeur Suisse Romande.

15.1 LFAIE Issue (Resolved)

The original company purpose included real estate activities (purchase, sale, promotion of property). The Geneva commercial register requires a LFAIE attestation (Federal Act on the Acquisition of Real Estate by Persons Abroad) for any company with real estate activities. This attestation costs CHF 500 and requires all members to produce their last two tax declarations, proof of Geneva domicile, and notarized attestations.

15.2 Decision (March 4, 2026)

Pascal decided to remove the real estate ownership/acquisition component to avoid the LFAIE requirement. The revised company purpose focuses on:

(1) Design, organization, marketing, and provision of tourism experiences in Switzerland; (2) Concierge, travel consulting, intermediation, and matchmaking services in tourism and bespoke travel; (3) Organization of stays, corporate retreats, and events; (4) Community management and membership programs for tourism and hospitality.

The company may also provide services related to the administration, rental, intermediation, operation, and maintenance of real estate properties — but explicitly excludes purchase, sale, and promotion of real estate in Switzerland.

15.3 Samy's Latest Response (March 5, 2026)

Samy removed real estate intermediation from the articles and requisition. He warns that even with the exclusion, the commercial register may still require a LFAIE attestation given the remaining real estate-related activities (administration, rental, maintenance). He asks Pascal to confirm whether he wants to remove ALL real estate activities or maintain the current revised scope.

15.4 Impact on Platform

The property management vertical (Section 2.2) depends on the final approved company purpose. If the Geneva register accepts the current scope, property management services can proceed as planned. If a LFAIE attestation is required, either the attestation must be obtained (CHF 500 + docs) or all real estate activities must be removed.
